Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries from prospective students and parents

Is PCME eligible for Engineering admissions?

Yes! PCME students are fully eligible for KCET, JEE, and all engineering entrance exams for branches like ECE, CSE, EEE, Mechanical, and AI/ML.

How does Electronics in PU help in higher studies?

Studying Electronics gives students a head start in first-year engineering and computer science, as they are already familiar with digital logic, circuit design, and hardware concepts.
